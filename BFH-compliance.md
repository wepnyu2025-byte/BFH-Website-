# BFH-COMPLIANCE.md — Baby First Health

This is a child-health education brand. These rules override design and copy preferences.

## Medical disclaimer (footer, every page)
"Baby First Health provides educational information and parenting support. Our content does not replace professional medical diagnosis, treatment or emergency care."

## Rules
1. Baby First Health is **not a hospital or clinic**. Never imply it provides diagnosis, treatment or emergency response.
2. Never write medical advice as a cure, treatment plan or dosage. Educational language only.
3. On any page about emergencies, health topics or the Emergency Guide, show a short note: "In an emergency, contact your nearest hospital or emergency services immediately."
4. **Certificates are private certificates of completion** issued by Baby First Health. Never call them government-recognized, licensed, accredited or a medical qualification. Wording: "Baby First Health certificate of completion."
5. Do not invent statistics, testimonials, reviews, member numbers, doctors, awards or partnerships.
6. Do not show prices, discounts or dates that the user has not supplied. The only stated benefit is the BF Benefits Card giving 15% savings on eligible Baby First Health products and services.
7. Do not use fear-based or shaming language toward parents. Tone: supportive, clear, practical.
8. Founder credentials appear only as written in `BFH-content.md` (Dolly Kelly, SRN; Wepnyu Laurence).
9. Contact form: state that messages should not be used for medical emergencies.
10. Images must never depict real medical procedures, injuries, or distressing scenes.
